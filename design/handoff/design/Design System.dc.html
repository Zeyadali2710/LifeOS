PIXEL ART RULES

Grid. 2:1 isometric, 32 × 16 px tiles. Characters 10 × 16 px, anchored bottom-centre on the chair tile. Desks 2 × 1 tiles. Scenes render at integer scale only (2× or 3×); avatars at 2–4×.

Palette. Day palette only in this direction: warm floor #dacfba, walls #e6ddcc, outline #2a2420. Screens carry status: teal working, amber needs-you, red failed, grey idle/paused.

Meaning. Bubbles (… ! × II) and the DOM label are the status. Typing, waving and smoke are decoration. Pixel art never contains text.

Motion. 4 fps ambient loop. Reduced motion (system or the in-page toggle) freezes on frame 0; selection becomes a solid ring.
